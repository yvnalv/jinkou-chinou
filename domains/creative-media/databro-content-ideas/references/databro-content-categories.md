# Databro Content Categories & Editorial Rotation Guide

This reference defines the 8 content categories used to plan, research, and rotate content across Databro's channels (@databroid / @databro.id).

---

## The 8 Core Editorial Categories

### 1. Machine Learning Concepts
- **Focus**: Algorithmic principles, architecture breakdowns, model evaluation, and MLOps.
- **Topics**: Gradient boosting vs neural nets, loss functions, ROC-AUC vs PR curves, data leakage, model quantization, drift detection, and MLflow pipelines.
- **Audience Goal**: Demystify complex ML theory into clean, visual mental models.

### 2. Data Science Workflow & Analysis
- **Focus**: End-to-end data exploration, data quality auditing, visualization best practices, and data storytelling.
- **Topics**: Systematic EDA, handling disguised nulls, Simpson's paradox, misleading chart axes, outlier treatment, and executive presentation design.
- **Audience Goal**: Build practical intuition for real-world messy tabular data.

### 3. AI News & Emerging Trends
- **Focus**: Fresh breakthroughs from the past 24 to 48 hours across the global AI ecosystem.
- **Topics**: Agentic AI patterns, tool calling architectures, open-weights foundation models, reasoning models, and production AI case studies.
- **Research Source**: arXiv papers, official AI lab announcements (Google DeepMind, Anthropic, OpenAI, Meta), GitHub trending repos, and engineering blogs.

### 4. RAG & Generative AI Techniques
- **Focus**: Production engineering for LLM-powered applications.
- **Topics**: Chunking strategies (semantic vs fixed-size), vector embeddings, dense vs sparse hybrid search, cross-encoder rerankers, context window management, and hallucination evaluation.
- **Audience Goal**: Practical engineering guides for builders moving beyond basic prompts.

### 5. Pure Math & Statistics Fundamentals
- **Focus**: The mathematical bedrock underlying machine learning and data science.
- **Topics**: Probability distributions (Normal, Poisson, Binomial), Central Limit Theorem, p-values and confidence intervals, Bayesian updating, linear algebra intuitions, and gradient descent calculus.
- **Audience Goal**: Ground learners in core math without intimidating academic jargon.

### 6. Hands-on Tutorials & How-To Guides
- **Focus**: Actionable, code-driven steps that readers can run on their laptops immediately.
- **Topics**: How to read a boxplot correctly, pandas 2.0 tricks, polars performance gains, matplotlib/seaborn formatting, regex for data cleaning, and automated web scraping.
- **Audience Goal**: Instant gratification with copy-pasteable snippets and clear visual diagrams.

### 7. Statistical & Real-World Datasets
- **Focus**: Deep-dives into authentic publicly available datasets.
- **Topics**: Developer salary surveys (Stack Overflow, Indonesian tech salary reports), BPS/census economic data, Spotify streaming metrics, ride-hailing datasets, and open Kaggle competitions.
- **Audience Goal**: Teach through real evidence rather than toy synthetic datasets.

### 8. Domain Analytics & Applied Statistics
- **Focus**: How data science creates business value outside traditional software engineering.
- **Topics**: Customer churn in telecommunications, fraud detection in fintech, supply chain forecasting in retail, hospital patient wait-time modeling, and A/B test analysis in growth marketing.
- **Audience Goal**: Connect data skills directly to business outcomes and career opportunities.

---

## Editorial Rotation Principles

1. **Avoid Category Monotony**: Never publish the same category two days in a row.
2. **Balance Evergreen vs Timely**:
   - Timely (Categories 3 & 7): Leverage current 24-48h news hooks.
   - Evergreen (Categories 1, 4, 5, 6, 8): Timeless fundamentals with high save and bookmark rates.
3. **Format Alignment**:
   - Math & Tutorials -> Best as Carousels (step-by-step slides).
   - AI Trends & News -> Best as Carousels or Video Reels (with `databro-brand-motion`).
   - Domain Case Studies -> Best as Carousels or LinkedIn Articles.
