# GLM Systems Brand Persona & Voice Guidelines

> **Company Profile**: GLM Systems (`glmsystems.com`) : Enterprise Software, ERP/CRM Automation, and Exact Synergy Solutions.
> **Brand Mascot & AI Assistant**: **Flippy** : The animated lime bird from the GLM logo that acts as the intelligent AI agent inside Exact Globe+ and Synergy.

---

## 1. Brand Personality & Tone of Voice

| Dimension | GLM Systems Identity | What We Sound Like | What We Never Sound Like |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Authority** | Senior Enterprise Solution Partner | Practical, authoritative, knowledgeable, grounded | Theoretical, hand-waving, academic |
| **Clarity** | Direct & Action-Oriented | Clear value statements, direct workflow savings | Corporate jargon, buzzword soup |
| **Energy** | Confident & Focused | Crisp, professional, reassuring | Screaming hype, chaotic, cartoonish |
| **Trust** | Enterprise Data Compliance | "Works within your rights. Never around them." | Reckless, black-box AI claims |

---

## 2. Language & Copywriting Rules

* **Primary Language**: Professional Business English (or Dutch for domestic Netherlands enterprise contexts).
* **Target Audience**: Exact Globe+ & Exact Synergy users, Financial Controllers, Operations Managers, ERP administrators, and B2B IT leadership.
* **The "Flippy" Narrative**:
  - Flippy is the friendly, agile lime bird that navigates complex ERP menus effortlessly.
  - Problem Hook: *"Still clicking through menus to find one sales order?"*
  - Solution Arrival: *"Meet Flippy. Your AI agent inside Exact Globe+ and Synergy."*
  - Core Promise: *"You don't search. You simply ask."*
  - Trust Anchor: *"Works within your rights. Never around them."*

---

## 3. Visual & Aesthetic Guardrails

* **Background**: Clean, crisp **Cool White (`#F2F0FA`)** or deep **Emerald (`#003A40`)**. Never muddy gray or chaotic gradients.
* **Typography & Palette**: Lime (`#CDFF70`), Emerald (`#003A40`), Stone Grey (`#444547`), Cool White (`#F2F0FA`). Headings in Poppins, wordmark in Geist.
* **Accent Highlights**: Vibrant **Lime (`#CDFF70`)** on Emerald or Dark text.
* **Pacing Standard**:
  - Minimum `0.4 seconds` per word for reading comprehension.
  - Minimum `2.0 seconds` on screen per text card.
  - At least `2.0 seconds` of stillness before transitioning to the next scene.
  - Outro endcard holds for at least `2.5 seconds`.
