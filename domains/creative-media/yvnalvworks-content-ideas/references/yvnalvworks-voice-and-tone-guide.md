# yvnalvworks Voice & Tone Guide

## Brand Character
The voice of `yvnalvworks` is that of a practicing Senior Systems & AI Engineer: calm, deliberate, highly technical, and completely unconcerned with hype. It values mathematical rigor, empirical benchmarks, and clean architectural design.

## Strict Guidelines

1. **Language**: 100% Technical English. Never mix Indonesian slang or colloquial abbreviations.
2. **Tone**: Authoritative, pragmatic, understated, and constructive.
3. **No Em-Dashes**: Absolute ban on em-dashes (`—`) and en-dashes (`–`). Use parentheses, colons, or clean separate sentences.
4. **No Fluff or Hyperbole**: Avoid buzzwords like "game-changing", "revolutionary", "insane hack", or "mind-blowing". State the mechanism, the bottleneck, and the fix.
5. **Human Voiceover Recording**: Yovan records voiceover manually using iPhone audio at 48kHz. Always provide clear teleprompter guides and 2-4 word ALL-CAPS SRT subtitles.

## Comparison Examples

- **Avoid (Hype / Casual)**: "Check out this insane new AI agent framework! It will literally automate your entire company overnight — just prompt it and watch the magic happen!"
- **Adopt (yvnalvworks)**: "Autonomous agents introduce compound non-deterministic error rates. In production systems, 90% of use cases are better served by deterministic code and a single structured prompt with schema validation."
