# Databro Studio (`@databroid`) — Brand Persona & Scriptwriting Guide

## 1. Brand Identity & Core Mission
- **Brand Name**: Databro (`@databroid`)
- **Tagline**: *Belajar Data Science*
- **Archetype**: The Practical Senior Data/AI Engineer "Bro" (Mentor yang santai, to-the-point, dan praktikal).
- **Mission**: Demystify Data Science, Machine Learning, and AI/LLM Engineering for Indonesian students, career switchers, and developers through bite-sized, visual, code-backed vertical videos.

---

## 2. Target Audience Persona
- **Who they are**: Indonesian university students, junior-to-mid software engineers, data analysts, and career switchers aged 18–32.
- **Pain Points**: Overwhelmed by theoretical jargon, confused about career paths (Data Analyst vs. Data Scientist vs. ML Engineer vs. LLM Engineer), and want practical tools they can run on their own laptop or cloud.
- **What hooks them**: Clear roadmaps, real terminal/code demos, salary/career insights, and fast 45–60 second explainers without fluff.

---

## 3. Tone of Voice & Language Rules (Indonesian + English Code-Switching)
1. **Conversational Indonesian + Natural English Tech Terms**:
   - Speak natural everyday Indonesian (*"Bro"*, *"kalian"*, *"kamu"*, *"nah"*, *"yuk"*, *"simpelnya"*), while keeping technical terms in English (`LLM Engineer`, `Prompt Engineering`, `RAG`, `Fine-Tuning`, `Vector Database`, `API`, `Guardrails`, `Latency`, `Python`).
   - Never over-translate standard engineering terms into awkward formal Indonesian (say `"model LLM"` or `"pipeline RAG"`, not *"rekayasa model bahasa besar"*).
2. **Signature Opening Hooks (`00:00–00:06`)**:
   - *"Bro, tahu nggak sih..."*
   - *"Bro, sering denger istilah [Topik], tapi bingung bedanya apa?"*
   - *"Sebenarnya kerjaan seorang [Role] itu ngapain aja sih?"*
3. **Signature Transitions (`Body`)**:
   - *"Simpelnya gini..."*
   - *"Yang pertama..."*, *"Kedua..."*, *"Ketiga..."*
   - *"Kalau kita lihat di terminal..."*
4. **Signature Sign-Off / CTA (`Outro`)**:
   - *"Gimana, tertarik jadi [Role]? Save video ini dan follow Databro buat belajar Data Science & AI bareng, bro!"*
   - *"Semoga bermanfaat, bro!"*

---

## 4. Content Pillars
1. **AI & Data Career Roadmaps**: What is an LLM Engineer, Data Engineer, ML Engineer, AI Engineer—skills, tools, and day-to-day workflows.
2. **Hands-On Local & Cloud AI Tutorials**: Running Ollama, Llama 3, Claude, Gemini, Vector DBs, and Python pipelines step-by-step.
3. **Concept Explainers in 60 Seconds**: RAG vs. Fine-Tuning, AI Agents, MCP, SQL optimization, and evaluation.
