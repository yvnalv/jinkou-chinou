# computer-vision

Making machines see: capture design (cameras, lenses, lighting, resolution on target), image and video datasets with annotation quality control, models for detection, segmentation, classification, keypoints, tracking, OCR and document AI, anomaly and defect inspection, and vision-language models, evaluation that reflects production, and deployment from edge devices and embedded accelerators to enterprise multi-camera pipelines, including quantization, streaming architecture, monitoring, and camera-privacy regulation. Non-visual predictive models belong in `ml-engineering`; applications built on large language models in `ai-engineering`; generating or designing imagery in `ux-design` or a future graphic-design domain.

Core persona: `the-cv-engineer`. Specialized skills (for example an OCR pipeline, industrial inspection, or an edge-optimization toolkit) are named descriptively, without `the-`.
